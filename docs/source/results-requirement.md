Deliverables – What You Submit
Each group is accountable for fulfilling their specific proposed deliverables. If some
promised deliverable is missed, then there must be a written explanation why it is not being
delivered.
The High-Level Project Plan
Major milestones completion – Turn in your completed project plan. It should have your
proposed completion dates as well as the actual dates. Be ready to discuss any major
deviations from the plan.
Written Documentation
The results that you proposed to deliver: proofs, solved problems, data, graphs, etc.
Computational/Technology Tools
Any result s you generated using technology/tools. Source code for any programs that you
committed to deliver.
Presentation
Draft of a PowerPoint that you intend to present to the class.
Quality Assurance Signoff
Test Results – Document verifying that all the results are correct and other deliverables
(e.g. presentation, programs, …) are error free.
Error analysis – If the Team finds an error in their results, the error should be documented.
Groups will lose less credit if they find their own mistakes. Groups will lose no credit if they
correctly explain how to fix their errors. (Real-life!)
Don’t forget:
You must coordinate with the instructor to schedule a time to present your results to
the class. You are strongly encouraged to schedule time with the Instructor to review
your results and in particular your presentation before presenting it to the class.
Project reflection:
Each student must submit an Individual Reflection write-up.
