# Simulation Convergence

Run at least 10,000 seasons per scenario, adding batches of 10,000 until mean-points Monte Carlo standard error is at most 0.1 point, each reported probability’s 95% Monte Carlo interval has at most a one-percentage-point half-width, and 80%/95% point-interval endpoints change by no more than 0.5 points across two consecutive checks. These criteria make the proposal’s minimum count measurable against the precision of reported results.
