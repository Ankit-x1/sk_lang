# Dallas Stars Season Forecast

This context covers the Math 3315 model of the Dallas Stars’ 2026-27 NHL regular season and its quality assurance.

## Language

**Goals for (GF) and goals against (GA)**:
Goals Dallas scores and concedes in a game. A shootout-deciding tally is not a hockey goal.

**Regulation result**:
A game outcome determined by the score at the end of the 60-minute regulation period.

**Overtime/shootout (OT/SO)**:
The extra-time procedures that decide a game tied after regulation.

**Overtime/shootout loss (OTL)**:
A loss in overtime or a shootout after regulation ends tied; it awards the losing team one standings point.

**Standings points**:
NHL regular-season points earned from game outcomes: two for a win, one for an OT/SO loss, and zero for a regulation loss.

**Simulation season**:
One modeled 82-game regular season.

**Baseline scenario**:
The reference forecast against which sensitivity and roster scenarios are compared.

**Sensitivity scenario**:
A forecast that changes offensive or defensive strength while holding the other side fixed.

**Roster scenario**:
A forecast representing a specified player addition, removal, or availability assumption, relative to a realistic replacement.

**Combined scenario**:
A plausible forecast that combines offensive, defensive, and roster assumptions.

**Backtest**:
A forecast of a completed season using only information available before that season, compared with the season’s actual results.

**Central Division translation**:
An estimate of how simulated Dallas standings points compare with historical Central Division point totals and standings outcomes.
